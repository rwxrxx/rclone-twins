Privacy policy

rclone-twins is a personal OAuth client for rclone, used only by its owner on their own computers.

  • It requests only the drive.file scope: access to the files and folders it has created itself, not to anything else in Google Drive.
  • Files are uploaded from the owner's computers to the owner's own Google Drive and are encrypted before they leave the computer.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, shared with or sold to anyone. There is no server behind this app.
  • OAuth tokens are kept only in the local rclone configuration on the owner's devices and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
